# Website Brand Brief

## Website Concept

Radiant Editorial Portfolio

## Core Throughline

Curiosity into clarity. Ideas into impact.

## Purpose

This is a recruiter-facing personal brand website for Shrujal Agarwal. It should feel like a creative extension of her resume, not a generic portfolio, student project, or resume copied onto a webpage.

The site should help recruiters, hiring managers, alumni, and referrals understand:

- Who Shrujal is
- How she thinks
- What roles she fits
- Why her mix of strategy, operations, analytics, marketing, leadership, and communication is valuable

## Personality To Preserve

- Warm
- Confident
- Radiant
- Ambitious
- Clear-minded
- People-oriented
- Strategic
- Execution-focused
- Premium
- Minimal
- Recruiter-friendly

## Design Style

Preserve the current design language:

- Premium editorial typography
- Warm ivory and beige palette
- Clean sticky navigation
- Soft luxury feel
- Large serif headings
- Clean sans-serif body text
- Photo-led storytelling
- Subtle flowing ribbon motif
- Muted terracotta, sage, clay, sand, and gold accents
- Generous but intentional spacing
- Recruiter-readable section flow

## Do Not Let Future Edits Make The Site Feel

- Generic
- Too corporate
- Too student-like
- Too image-heavy
- Too abstract
- Too cluttered
- Too empty
- AI-generated
- Like a school album
- Like an Instagram gallery

## Quality Bar

The final site should feel custom-built, elegant, warm, human, recruiter-friendly, and memorable. Every section should support the throughline: curiosity becomes clarity, and clarity becomes impact.
